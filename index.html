XPostOptimizer

Simulate the X algorithm's Phoenix transformer for post optimization. Enter your post details to get estimated engagement probabilities and suggestions.

    Disclaimer: This is an unofficial simulator based solely on the open-sourced xai-org/x-algorithm repo. It approximates the Phoenix Grok-based transformer (with candidate isolation masking for independent post scoring) and home-mixer weighted scoring. Results are heuristic estimates focusing on engagement velocity, originality, and media signals—not official or guaranteed. No data is sent to servers.
